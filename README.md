The funniest one-line review of the entrant's own Monday.

## The entry

> Monday: one star. My inbox and my out-of-office auto-replied to each other, and the 9 AM standup marked me absent while I was on camera.

136 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject and funny** — a review of the entrant's own Monday, opened as a product rating: "Monday: one star."
2. **A real punchline, in the last clause** — the 9 AM standup marked the reviewer absent while they were on camera: present and erased at the same time.
3. **Concrete details, not generic tiredness** — an inbox and an out-of-office auto-replying to each other, and the 9 AM standup. The loop is phrased as mutual ("to each other") so the image lands on first read, with no re-reading needed to work out who replied to whom.
4. **No wasted words** — two details, one verdict, one punchline; the turn arrives before the reader gives up.
5. **Repeatable** — "marked me absent while I was on camera" is the sentence a reader quotes to a coworker the next morning.
